Day 18:
Day 18 - Performance Profiling
Capture (phone->laptop via WiFi): ~29.6ms avg (jittery, ranged 10-90ms per-frame)
HSV convert: ~0.9ms avg
Mask (inRange): ~0.7ms avg
Contour find: ~0.5ms avg

Bottleneck: Capture (WiFi network hop), by far — 15-40x the cost of all CV processing combined
Takeaway: CV processing itself is nearly free; the real cost is getting the frame off the phone over WiFi in the first place. 
It confirms the final on-phone pipeline design (no laptop hop) should eliminate this bottleneck entirely.
